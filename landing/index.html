FAQ

Before you buy or install

Is this a browser tool or a desktop app?

Cinekive is a desktop app for Windows, Mac, and Linux. This page is the marketing site. A read-only browser gallery may exist at app.cinekive.ai. Download the free installer for search, ingest, and moodboards.

How do Free and Pro differ?

Free runs your archive forever: search, ingest, canvas, single exports, local AI models. Free includes 3 projects; Pro removes the cap. Pro adds studio tools: cloud AI keys, lookbook PNG/PDF, folder auto-ingest, batch export, sync, email support. Pro is Annual at $19/year or Lifetime at $99 once.

How does the free trial work?

Email cinekive@agentmail.to for a no-card 14-day key. It expires on its own and the app stays on Free Desktop. A card-on-file yearly trial is not on sale yet. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no trial.

How does license activation work?

Buy Lifetime on Lemon Squeezy. You get a license key by email. In the app: Settings → Cinekive Pro → paste the key. There is no Cinekive account. The app checks the key with Lemon Squeezy (key and instance id only, not your media). Each key has a device limit set on the store (default 3). Online re-check about every 14 days. Offline grace is 14 days. After that only Pro tools lock. Free Desktop still works. Lost key: look it up in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.

Is my media uploaded?

Not by default. The library stays on your disk. License checks send the key and product id only. Pro features send media off-machine only when you choose them (a share link, or a cloud model with your own API key).

Does Cinekive include other companies' stills?

No. The installer ships a handful of public-domain stills as offline placeholders, nothing else. You add files you have the right to use. Cinekive does not clear rights for you — use only assets you're authorized to use.

Can it generate images?

Tagging is the core. It labels real stills. Pro can generate a new still from a reference if you supply your own image-model key. That path is optional.
